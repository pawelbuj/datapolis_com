Processes are the system of record

Not AI. Not apps. The process defines who does what, when, and under what rules. Everything else — humans, AI, interfaces — works inside that structure.

Humans act

Make decisions, grant approvals, handle exceptions. People stay in control where it matters.

AI acts

Execute tasks, process data, assist users. Digital workers operate inside the same governance layer.

Process governs

Rules, ownership, routing, and audit trail. The process is the single source of truth.
