THE SPECIFICATION VIEW

Define what the business system means.

A useful application specification goes beyond a feature list. It describes the decisions the running system must make and control.

  • Goals and success conditions.
  • Roles, responsibilities and ownership.
  • Business data and relationships.
  • Process states, actions and exceptions.
  • Permissions and acceptance scenarios.

Structured, not frozen

The specification remains connected to native application objects.

THE APPLICATION VIEW

Design and run the working system.

The same model becomes a no-code application that teams can inspect, change and publish without translating it into a separate implementation language.

  • State-based processes coordinate work.
  • Data structures hold business context.
  • Pages adapt to role and state.
  • Permissions govern every participant.
  • Digital Workers execute explicit tasks.

SYNCHRONIZATION

A change is a controlled model operation.

2to2 shows the consequences of a change before it updates the working application or its specification.

01

Detect

Identify which model objects and business statements are affected.

02

Explain

Present the impact in language analysts and designers can review.

03

Apply

Update native objects through a controlled build and publishing step.

The goal is not better documentation. The goal is software that cannot silently drift away from what the business defined.
